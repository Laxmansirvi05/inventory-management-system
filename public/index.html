Up and running in three steps

No spreadsheets to merge, nothing to install.

  1. Set up your shop

    Add categories, suppliers and customers, then your products with prices, photos and a minimum stock level.

  2. Run the day

    Record purchases when stock arrives and sales at the counter. Every figure updates straight away.

  3. Stay ahead

    Check the restock alerts each morning, review the reports each week, and print a bill whenever a customer asks.
